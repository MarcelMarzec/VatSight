**To track a friend on VATSIM you need one thing: their VATSIM CID.** Callsigns change from flight to flight, but a CID is a member’s permanent ID number, so once you have it you can find them whenever they connect, whether they’re flying or controlling. This guide shows how to get it and how to follow them on your iPhone with [VatSight](/vatsim-tracker).

## Step 1: Get your friend’s CID

A CID (certificate ID) is the number VATSIM gives every member when they register. There are a few ways to get your friend’s:

- **Ask them.** It’s shown when they sign in at my.vatsim.net, and most pilot and ATC software has it in its settings.
- **Find them while they’re online.** Search VatSight for their callsign or their name, open their details, and the CID is the number in brackets after their name.
- **From an airport.** If you know where they’re flying from or to, tap that airport and look through its departures and arrivals.

> [!note] Is this private information?
> No. VATSIM publishes the name, CID, position and flight plan of everyone connected in its public network data, which is how every VATSIM map works. Following someone’s CID shows you nothing that isn’t already public.

## Step 2: Add your own CID

Open **Settings → Friends** and enter your own CID under **Your CID**. It’s optional, but worth it:

- Your aircraft shows in **gold** on the map, so you can always find yourself.
- While you’re connected, a **locate** button appears on the map that flies straight to your aircraft, or to your airport or sector when you’re controlling.

## Step 3: Add your friends

There are three ways to add someone:

1. **By CID.** In **Settings → Friends**, type their CID into **Friend’s VATSIM CID** and add it.
2. **From a pilot’s details.** Tap their aircraft on the map, or find them in search, and tap the **star** next to their name.
3. **From a controller.** Open an airport or sector, and **swipe right** on the controller to add them.

Your friends list lives only on your phone. VatSight has no accounts and no server, so nobody else can see who you follow.

## Step 4: See who’s online

The Friends list doubles as a who’s-online board. Each friend shows as:

- **Flying**, with their callsign, route and how long they’ve been connected.
- **Controlling**, with their callsign, frequency and time online.
- **Offline**.

Tap any online friend and the map flies straight to them.

![VatSight live map of the United Kingdom, where friends' aircraft and sectors are highlighted in green](/images/screenshots/uk-overview-750.webp "Friends show up in green on the live map")

On the map itself, friends stand out in **green**: their aircraft, the labels of any sectors they’re controlling, and the airports they’re working. Tapped aircraft show in red, and you in gold, so the three never get confused.

## Tips for following a group flight or event

- **Add everyone before the event.** It takes seconds per CID, and the Friends list then shows at a glance who has connected.
- **Use the airport sheet.** On a group fly-in, tap the destination to see everyone inbound, with a line from the airport to each aircraft.
- **Turn on the altitude filter** and tap a friend’s aircraft: the slider jumps to their level, so you only see the sectors they’re flying through, and know which controller they’re talking to.
