## Planned features
status: planned
date: Future

Better flight following, Navigraph integration, METAR and ATIS on the map, and push notifications. These will be spread across future updates.

- Follow your flight, with the current sector and a prediction of the next one
- Navigraph integration, including navdata and charts
- Fully rendered routes, including waypoints, airways, SIDs and STARs
- More detailed airport charts displayed directly on the map
- A visual representation of the current weather
- Bug fixing

## v1.1.1 - Events and Bookings Update
status: development
date: Up next

VATSIM events and ATC bookings, in the app and on the map.

- Events tab with every current and upcoming VATSIM event, as a list or a timeline
- Full event details: banner, times, airports, routes, organisers and the ATC booked for the event
- Filters by event type, region and division, remembered between launches
- Live and upcoming event airports highlighted on the map
- Booked ATC shown in each airport's details, and booked airspace outlined on the map
- Times in UTC, local time or both
- Subscribe to the VATSIM events calendar
- Map legend, and fading of airports and aircraft that aren't connected to the airport you've selected
